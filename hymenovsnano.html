Still Schizomantodea ?

There are a LOT of species in Schizomantodea, so theres more ways to find out what your mantis is.


Mantises in general are predators, they are insectivores who depend on hunting their prey to get their nutrients. They use their raptorial forelegs to catch their prey and keep it steady while they eat.

Different species have taken different approaches to hunt down their prey. Its kind of like preparing for a competiton, everyone has different strengths, and these have adapted to have different ways to excell in the wild.


Agressive Mimicry

Hymenomantodea

Usually not from appearance, but many who partake in 'agressive' mimicry tend to be a lot more colourful, and display themselves as more colourful critters. They do this to seem harmless, so that approaching prey can result easier.

This family usually is larger in size and much more colourful.

Image sourced from Wikipedia.


Nanomantidae

As the name suggests, 'nano' means small. A lot of these species tend to be severely small, as in, nearing 30mm or less! These are usually also able to take on agressive mimicry, but they tend to be less agressive. They mimic leaves, lichen, moss and bark, and usually are great at camoflague.

This family usually is smaller in size and less noticably colourful.
