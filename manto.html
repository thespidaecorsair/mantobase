Your species is most likely in...

Mantidae

This group of mantids make up 80% of the world's population of mantises!


My mantis has a longer 'neck' (pronotal) OR said pronotal has a very leaf/bark-like shape.

Many mantids have adapted to use every part of their appearance as something to help them camoflague. These especially have adapted to look like sticks, leaves, and anything that can otherwise appear harmless to their predators and prey alike.

Images sourced from iNaturalist (@nickvolpe, @connor_margetts)


My mantis is brown/green and has no main camoflaguing protrusions.
