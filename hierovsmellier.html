Your species is most likely in...

Hierodulinae OR Mellierinae

These two taxa share a lot in common, and it primarily depends on smaller features that they have.

My mantis is bulkier and a lot less skinny than expected.

Mantises in Heirodulinae tend to be thicker, and less like a slim stick. They also tend to be larger, where Heirodulinae species are considered medium to large, Mellerinae are considered just medium sized. They are a colourful type of mantid usually, especially as this taxa can also be found in more tropical areas of Africa and Asia, their colourful features helps for better adaption to the plants and surroundings there.

Images sourced from iNaturalist (@sable, @mathew_connors, @ronigreer)


My mantis is slimmer and longer, and not as bulky as the others shown.

(Though do keep in mind, females tend to be larger and have a more pronounced abdomen than men, so make sure you know what is meant by 'bulky'.)

The mantids of Mellerinae tend to be a lot more fit for camoflague in the Australian environment, as they are exclusively an Australian species. They are not as colourful as Hierodulinae.

It can help to double check on reliable websites that have image-identifying AIs like iNaturalist to make sure you are identifying the specific species you want.
