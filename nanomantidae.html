Your species is most likely in...

Nanomantidae

These mantises are significantly small, as one could assume from 'nano' in its name. There are a lot of different types of nanomantids.


My mantis has net-like wings and/or translucent body with spots/stripes.

These mantises are recognisable for their net-like wings, similar to lacewings (neuroptera). They are small, as all nanomantids, and even when they are not winged (still nymphs), their body has that same transluscent texture to them with stripes and dots, almost.

Images sourced from iNaturalist (@graemvc, @squiresk)


My mantis resembles bark, wood or tree structures, and its abdomen has leaf/bark-like portrusions.

These are also in nanomantidae, but instead blend into their surroundigns by mimicing wood. Do be careful, as you might mix these up with other species! They all can look very similar if you are not used to observing mantises frequently.

Images sourced from iNaturalist (@ccborneo, @dustaway)


My mantis resembles bark, wood or tree structures, and is striped and slim.

This mantid can be similar to a lot of existing species you have passed, but they are recognisable for their striped patterns and how it helps them mimic their surroundings.

Images sourced from iNaturalist (@reiner, @suecar)
