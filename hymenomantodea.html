Hymenopodidae - Typical Flower Mantids

'Hymeno' is used to describe the idea of membranous wings, as seen with species in Hymenoptera (bees, ants, wasps). This species of mantis is usually colourful and well decorated ,ready to blend in with otherwise harmless, approachable parts of an ecosystem to trick their prey and predators alike. This is considered agressive mimicry due to its outcome.
