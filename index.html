What is Mantodea?

Mantodea is the order for mantids, also commonly known as "praying mantises". Known for their remarkable camoflague, resembing sticks, twigs or leaves. Many younger nymphs (young mantises, before adulthood) mimic ants (myrmecomorphy).

Australian Museum, 30.08.2024

Learn more about . . . Myrmecomorphy

Mantises are a vital part of the insect world, with raptorial forelegs adapted to catch prey. Additionally, some species can turn their necks up to 180°. But don't worry, they are completely harmless to humans!

Wikipedia, 20.05.2026
