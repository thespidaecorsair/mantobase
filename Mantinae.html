Your species is most likely in...

Paraoxypilina

This is a subtribe of mantids (from the family nanomantidae), usually called "Spiny Bark Mantids". They're usually of a small to medium size, and have cryptic colours to blend in to treebark. Some also mimic ants, (Myrmecomorphy). You can come read about this in the information page!
