Your species is most likely in...

Tenoderinae OR Mantinae

These two taxa share a lot in common, and it primarily depends on smaller features that they have.

My mantis has a longer neck and has clear wings.

The mantids of Tenoderinae tend to have longer necks than expected, especially as 'Mantinae' is considered the most well known or 'true' version of the mantids. They also rarely have tinted forewings, unlike mantinae, though there are some exceptions. The images below display clear forewings. Alongside this, they also do not usually have clear darkened membrane lines on their forewings.
